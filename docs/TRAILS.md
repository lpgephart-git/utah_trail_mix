# Utah Trail Mix — Verified Wasatch Front Trails

*Researched 2026-08-11. Verify parking/conditions again the week of each hike.*

## ⚠️ Important 2026 change: Cottonwood Canyon parking fees

As of **June 1, 2026**, Big & Little Cottonwood Canyons are **Recreation Enhancement Act
(REA) fee areas** — parking (including roadside, within posted boundaries) requires a
valid **day-use pass or accepted federal recreation pass**. Applies to Donut Falls, Lake
Blanche, Cecret Lake/Albion Basin, Silver Lake, etc. Parking tickets run $75–$150.
→ For any canyon hike, tell attendees to buy a pass in advance and/or **carpool** (fewer
cars = fewer passes). Foothill trails below have **no** such fee.

## Tier 1 — Easy / foothill (free parking, great for kickoff & mixed groups)

| Trail | Distance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Ensign Peak** | ~1.0 mi RT | Iconic valley view, 1 mi N of the Capitol, free parking. Short = perfect low-commitment kickoff. |
| **Living Room** | ~2.3 mi RT | Foothills above the U; big valley/Great Salt Lake views. Trailhead lot off Wasatch Blvd has ~133 stalls. |
| **City Creek / Memory Grove** | flexible | Shaded canyon paths right by downtown/Capitol; easy, very accessible. |

## Tier 2 — Moderate (fall-color season, needs canyon pass)

| Trail | Distance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Cecret Lake, Albion Basin** | ~1.7 mi RT (or 4.5 w/ approach) | Alpine lake + legendary wildflowers (peak July); LCC — **REA pass**. |
| **Donut Falls** | ~3.5 mi RT | Family-friendly waterfall, BCC — **REA pass**. |
| **Silver Lake (Brighton) boardwalk** | ~0.9 mi loop | Flat, accessible, gorgeous; BCC top — **REA pass**. |

## Tier 3 — Ambitious (for the eager crowd)

| Trail | Distance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Lake Blanche** | ~6.5 mi RT, ~2,700 ft | Steep but stunning; BCC — **REA pass**. |
| **Mount Olympus** | ~7 mi RT, ~4,000 ft | Wasatch granite (NOT red rock — fix that mockup photo); strenuous. |

## Seasonality

Fall foliage on the Wasatch Front peaks **late September → mid-October** — ideal window
for a color-focused October hike.

## Recommended kickoff (Sept 2026)

**Ensign Peak.** Easy, short, iconic view, free parking, welcoming to all fitness levels
— exactly the low-pressure "come meet people" vibe you want for hike #1. Pick a Saturday
morning; follow with coffee nearby (the mockup's coffee-after idea is great for
networking). Living Room is the slightly-longer backup.

*Sources: Bearfoot Theory, KSL, Visit Salt Lake, UDOT Cottonwood Canyons, Trails Utah.*
